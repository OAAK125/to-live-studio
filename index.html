Services

From first sketch to working demo.

01

UI/UX Design

Thoughtful interfaces and user flows that help make a product idea clear, usable, and visually compelling.

  • Wireframes
  • User flows
  • Interface design
  • Responsive screen designs
  • Clickable prototypes
02

Interactive Prototyping

Clickable experiences that help you communicate a concept, explore user journeys, and demonstrate a product before investing in full development.

  • Linked screens
  • Simulated interactions
  • User journey demonstrations
  • Pitch-ready prototypes
03

Coded Product Demos

Responsive front-end experiences that turn approved designs into something people can see, click, and explore in a browser.

  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript where appropriate
  • React projects as separate engagements
  • Responsive layouts
  • Front-end interactions
  • Source-code handover

Prototypes and demos illustrate an idea. They are not production-ready software, and a basic demo does not include backends, payments, authentication, or databases.

About the studio

Design thinking meets hands-on building.

To Live Studio brings together product design, user experience, and front-end development to help turn early-stage ideas into digital experiences people can see, explore, and interact with.

The approach is design-led: structure and visual direction come first, then AI-assisted development speeds up the move from approved screens to a working front end you can click through. It suits founders, small businesses, developers, and agencies who need a concept made visible.
